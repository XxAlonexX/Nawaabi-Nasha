THE CRAFTSMANSHIP

The Art of the Betel

True luxury cannot be rushed. It resides in the deliberate pace of manual labor—the gentle hand-pressing of gulkand made from sun-dried Damask roses, and the meticulous selection of vintage mewa aged to perfection.

Our betel leaves are sourced from heritage groves where the soil is tended with organic compost and the water is filtered through copper channels, ensuring a leaf of unparalleled suppleness and flavor profile.
